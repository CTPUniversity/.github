# ABOUT CTP UNIVERSITY

**CTP University** is a private university dedicated to advancing education and research in the transformative sciences of consciousness, energy, and multidimensional reality.

Rooted in **CTP Energy Science (CTPSci)**, the University develops a structured body of academic programs exploring the relationship between consciousness, energy, physical reality, and the broader dimensions of existence. Its academic framework integrates CTP Energy Science, Conscious Translational Physics, non-physical sciences, consciousness studies, postquantum sciences, hyperspatial sciences, and emerging technologies.

The University's curriculum is designed to provide progressive study ranging from foundational principles to advanced and emerging fields. The current **CTPSci curriculum comprises 122 courses across three phases**, with additional academic programs and fields of study under development.

CTP University advances an educational framework in which scientific inquiry, consciousness, energy, and emerging technologies are studied as interconnected dimensions of a broader understanding of reality.

Areas of study include **CTP Energy Science, Conscious Translational Physics, consciousness and cognition, C-domain sciences, non-physical sciences, postquantum sciences, hyperspatial sciences, CTP gravitic propulsion, extraterrestrial sciences, interstellar sciences, species and civilizations, ontological architecture, accelerated intelligence, longevity, and emerging CTP technologies**.

CTP University is an evolving private academic institution established to develop, organize, and advance this expanding body of knowledge through structured curricula, specialized courses, research, and higher education.
