# CTP University

**CTP University** is the educational institution of **CTP Energy, LLC**, established to develop, organize, and advance education surrounding CTP Energy Science (CTPSci), Conscious Translational Physics, and the expanding body of knowledge, sciences, technologies, and applications emerging from the CTP framework.

CTP University provides a structured educational environment for the study of CTP Energy Science and related disciplines, bringing together foundational studies, advanced scientific concepts, interdisciplinary exploration, research-oriented learning, and the development of new fields of study.

## 🎓 Academic Programs

The University's initial academic foundation is the **CTP Energy Science (CTPSci) Curriculum**, comprising a progressively structured body of courses spanning foundational, advanced, and future-oriented areas of study.

### CTP Energy Science (CTPSci)

The current CTPSci curriculum comprises **122 courses across three phases**:

* **Phase I — Foundational Studies:** 8 courses introducing the fundamental principles and concepts of CTPSci.
* **Phase II — Advanced Studies:** 96 courses extending CTPSci into advanced scientific, technological, consciousness, planetary, civilizational, and interdisciplinary domains.
* **Phase III — Future Studies:** 18 courses exploring advanced and emerging concepts associated with higher-dimensional systems, post-human development, C-domain consciousness, and related areas.

The curriculum is an evolving body of work and will expand as additional courses, fields of study, research, and educational programs are developed.
